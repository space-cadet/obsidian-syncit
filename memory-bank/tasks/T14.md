## Goal

Replace the simple "Sync Now" button with a sync direction dropdown and add default policy settings to the Settings UI. This makes sync direction explicit and eliminates friction for common use cases (backup vs. sync vs. restore).

## Background

T13 implemented a reconciliation panel for ambiguous sync situations, but it adds complexity for users who just want a simple "push to server" or "pull from server" workflow. The reconciliation panel will be temporarily disabled in favor of explicit sync directions, then brought back later with proper default policies.
