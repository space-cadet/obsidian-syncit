## Problem Statement

Sync is unacceptably slow due to four issues: two bugs (missing nested files, sequential-only execution) and two bottlenecks (redundant MKCOL, no local sync index). A vault with even 50 files takes tens of seconds because every file is handled one-at-a-time, and subfolders are silently skipped entirely.
