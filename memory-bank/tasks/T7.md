*Created: 2026-08-17 14:20 IST*

**Status**: 📋 **PLANNED**
**Priority**: P1 (User's #4 — new, not in original list)

## Details

Ability to pause sync temporarily and resume later. Critical for situations where you don't want auto-sync interfering (e.g., making bulk changes, on slow connection, or during a presentation).

## Goals

1. **Pause/Resume button**: In sidebar and ribbon
2. **Pause duration options**: 
   - Pause until resumed (manual)
   - Pause for 1 hour
   - Pause until next restart
3. **Queue changes while paused**: Track what changed during pause, sync on resume
4. **Visual indicator**: Clear "PAUSED" status in sidebar/status bar
5. **Command**: "Pause sync" / "Resume sync" commands
